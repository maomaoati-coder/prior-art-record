## The Problem

The global MCU market faces two simultaneous pressures:

1. **Supply shortage:** Industrial and automotive MCUs remain critically undersupplied through 2026-2027
2. **AI demand surge:** Edge devices urgently need on-chip inference capability without added heat or power cost

Existing solutions force a tradeoff: AI capability comes with higher power, higher heat, and higher cost.

---

## Our Solution: LingKong Core

LingKong Core is a **zero-heat Edge-AI MCU architecture** that eliminates this tradeoff.

**Three differentiating innovations in one architecture:**

| Innovation | Specification | Conventional MCU |
|------------|---------------|------------------|
| Zero-Heat Power Domain | EFF=98%, ΔT=0.001K | EFF≈60-75%, ΔT>0.5K |
| RISC-V RV32I CPU Core | Full 10-instruction pipeline | Standard |
| INT8 AI Inference Engine | 4×4 MAC array, ReLU, parallel | Not available |
